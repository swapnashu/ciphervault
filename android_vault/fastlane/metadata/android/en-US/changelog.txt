Initial release.

- Zero-knowledge vault: Argon2id key derivation, AES-256-GCM encryption, sync
  against a server that can only store ciphertext.
- Passwords, usernames, URLs, notes and 2FA secrets in one vault.
- Built-in TOTP authenticator with QR-code enrolment.
- Password generator with strength meter.
- Android autofill service and strong-biometric unlock.
- Auto-lock after two minutes in the background, certificate pinning, and
  screenshot/recording blocking.
