CipherVault is a zero-knowledge password manager. Your vault is encrypted and
decrypted on this phone with your master password - the server only ever stores
ciphertext it cannot read.

Why zero-knowledge matters
The server never receives your master password or your vault keys. If someone
breaches the server, they get encrypted blobs and nothing else. There is no
password reset, because nobody but you can open your vault - that is the point.

Security built in
- Argon2id key derivation: memory-hard key derivation resists GPU and ASIC
  attacks, not just fast hash cracking.
- AES-256-GCM encryption with keys derived on device.
- Strong biometrics only (Class 3): fingerprint or face unlock can be enabled
  after you have unlocked once with your master password.
- Certificate pinning: the app refuses to talk to a look-alike server even if a
  certificate authority is tricked.
- Auto-lock: the vault locks itself after two minutes in the background.
- FLAG_SECURE: screenshots, screen recording and the recents preview are blocked
  so credentials cannot leave the display.
- Autofill: fill and save credentials in other apps through the Android autofill
  service.

Everyday features
- Store passwords, usernames, URLs, notes and card details.
- Built-in authenticator: paste or scan a Base32 secret and get 60-second TOTP
  codes without a second app.
- Generate strong passwords with a live strength meter.
- Search, favourites and categories to keep large vaults usable.
- Copy any field to the clipboard with one tap.

Honest about the trade-off
There is no password reset link and no cloud recovery. Write your master
password down and keep it somewhere safe - the app tells you this before you
create your vault, not after.

No ads, no trackers, no analytics. The app needs the internet only to sync your
encrypted vault.
